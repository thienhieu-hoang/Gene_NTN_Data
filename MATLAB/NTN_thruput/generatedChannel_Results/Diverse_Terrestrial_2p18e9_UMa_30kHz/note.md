# Dataset Overview: Diverse Terrestrial 3GPP TR 38.901 Channel (2.18 GHz, UMa, 30 kHz)

## General Information

- **Dataset Type:** Physically Grounded Terrestrial Channel Dataset (3GPP TR 38.901 UMa)
- **Data Generator:** MATLAB 5G Toolbox (`Gen_Chan_Diverse_Terrestrial_Dataset.m`)
- **Total Samples:** 20000 drops (Train: 16000, Val: 2000, Test: 2000)
- **Available Files:** `H_train.npy` (16000), `H_val.npy` (2000), `H_test.npy` (2000)
- **Data Type:** `complex64`, shape `(N, 132, 14)` (132 subcarriers × 14 OFDM symbols)
- **Power Normalization:** Raw unnormalized physical channel scale preserved

## Physical Justification & Domain Gap vs. NTN

1. **No Satellite Doppler Pre-compensation**: In NTN, the gNB/UE pre-compensates for the massive orbital Doppler shift, leaving only slight residual Doppler. In terrestrial, the BS is stationary, and all Doppler is generated by user mobility with an isotropic (360°) Jakes arrival spectrum.
2. **Higher Delay Spread (Stronger Frequency Selectivity)**: Due to rich terrestrial building clutter and multi-bounce scatterers, delay spread reaches 150–800 ns (vs 30–150 ns in NTN LOS), significantly reducing coherence bandwidth.
3. **Predominance of NLOS Rayleigh Fading**: While NTN at medium-to-high elevations is dominated by strong LOS paths ($K \approx 5-18$ dB), terrestrial UMa is predominantly NLOS (~70–80% NLOS) with deep Rayleigh fading nulls across subcarriers.
